# About Phys-Notes

A collection of letters written to young physicists, inspired by photos taken
from everday life.

## Post structure
* Title: 1-6 words. Noun phrase describing the raw observation. APA title case.
* Slug: Title in snake case.
* Published At: Date of handwritten note, any timestamp.
* Picture: Ideally square.
* Alt text: Super-brief, minimally describing the overall view. Lowercase,
  no sentence punctuation.
* Caption: Entirely descriptive. No "notice". Full sentences.
* Note: A letter to a young physicist, by name, may contain figures.
* Note link: text is anything, hyperlink is `#fig-N`
* Figure: name `fig-N`
* Original letter pages caption: "original handwritten note [p. N]"
